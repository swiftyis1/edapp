## Part 1: B.LS3.2 (Genetic Variation Viable Errors) UI Validation

### 1. DOK 1: Meiosis Stage Match
1. **Select Standard:** Choose **OAS B.LS3.2: Genetic Variation Viable Errors** in the top dropdown.
2. **Select DOK:** Click **DOK 1**.
3. **Answer Quiz:** Answer the 3-question DOK 1 checklist quiz.
4. **Verify Workspace Unlocked:** Scroll down to the **Meiosis Stage Match** workspace.
5. **Interact:** Select the correct meiotic stage for each event from the dropdowns:
   * **Homologous chromosomes align & cross over:** `Prophase I`
   * **Sister chromatids separate to poles:** `Anaphase II`
   * **Homologous pairs separate to poles:** `Anaphase I`
6. **Verify Completion:** Click **Verify Meiosis Stages**. Ensure status displays **Complete**.

### 2. DOK 2: Mutation Type Match
1. **Select DOK:** Click **DOK 2**.
2. **Answer Quiz:** Answer the 3 checklist questions.
3. **Interact:** Scroll down to the **Mutation Type Match** workspace. Match mutation events:
   * **Insertion of single base shifting reading frame:** `Frameshift Mutation`
   * **C to T transition changing codon to Stop:** `Nonsense Mutation`
   * **Substitution changing codon but keeping same amino acid:** `Silent Mutation`
4. **Verify Completion:** Click **Verify Mutations**. Ensure status displays **Complete**.

### 3. DOK 3: Crossing Over Sandbox
1. **Select DOK:** Click **DOK 3**.
2. **Answer Quiz:** Answer the 3 checklist questions.
3. **Interact:** Scroll down to the **Crossing Over Sandbox** workspace. Calibrate sliders:
   * **Chiasmata Count:** Slide to exactly `2 chiasmata`
   * **Meiotic Spindle Tension:** Slide to exactly `70%`
4. **Verify Completion:** Click **Verify Crossing Over**. Ensure status displays **Complete**.

### 4. DOK 4: Mutation Survival Optimizer
1. **Select DOK:** Click **DOK 4**.
2. **Answer Quiz:** Answer the 3 checklist questions.
3. **Interact:** Scroll down to the **Mutation Survival Optimizer** workspace. Calibrate parameters:
   * **Mutagen Dosage:** Slide to exactly `5 rads`
   * **DNA Repair Efficiency:** Slide to exactly `90%`
4. **Verify Completion:** Click **Verify Survival Planner**. Ensure status displays **Complete**.
